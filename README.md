This pizza ordering application is intended for people who want a quick and easy way to order pizza from home. The main users would be busy individuals, students, and families who may not have the time or desire to cook or go out to pick up food. It is also useful for customers who order pizza regularly and want the convenience of saving their past orders instead of having to choose their pizza preferences every time. Customers can use the application when they want a quick meal, are having a busy day, or simply want pizza delivered to their home. The application is designed to be simple and easy to use for people who may not be familiar with ordering food online.
